# Acme Corp Employee Expense Policy (v1.0)

All amounts are in INR. Claims in other currencies are converted using the rates in the finance configuration.

## 1. General Rules
1.1 Claims must be submitted within 90 days of the expense date.
1.2 Every claim must include the date, category, amount, currency and a description of the business purpose.
1.3 Personal expenses are not reimbursable, even if paid on a company trip.
1.4 Claims that look unusual or unclear must be referred to a reviewer.

## 2. Meals
2.1 Business meals while travelling are reimbursable up to INR 1,500 per claim.
2.2 Meals during normal working days at the home office are not reimbursable, except for approved team events.
2.3 Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## 3. Travel and Transport
3.1 Economy-class flights and standard trains are reimbursable when travel is for approved business purposes.
3.2 Local taxis, cabs and ride-hailing for business travel are reimbursable up to INR 3,000 per claim.
3.3 Daily commuting between home and the regular office is not reimbursable.
3.4 Personal vehicle use is reimbursed at INR 12 per kilometre with a trip log.

## 4. Lodging
4.1 Hotel stays for approved business travel are reimbursable up to INR 8,000 per night.
4.2 Lodging claims must include the number of nights and the city.
4.3 Room upgrades, minibar and entertainment charges are not reimbursable.

## 5. Software and Subscriptions
5.1 Software and subscriptions needed for work are reimbursable up to INR 5,000 per claim if approved by a manager in advance.
5.2 Personal or non-work subscriptions are not reimbursable.

## 6. Client Entertainment
6.1 Client meals and entertainment are reimbursable up to INR 6,000 per claim.
6.2 The client name and the business purpose must be stated in the description.
6.3 Gifts to clients above INR 2,000 require prior written approval.

## 7. Office Supplies and Equipment
7.1 Small office supplies are reimbursable up to INR 2,500 per claim.
7.2 Equipment above INR 10,000 requires prior approval through procurement and is not reimbursable as an expense claim.

## 8. Training and Conferences
8.1 Work-related courses and conference fees are reimbursable up to INR 25,000 per claim with manager approval.
8.2 Training must be relevant to the employee's role.

## 9. Receipts
9.1 A receipt is required for every claim above INR 500.
9.2 Claims at or below INR 500 may be submitted without a receipt with a short explanation.
9.3 Missing receipts above the threshold must be explained and approved by a reviewer.

## 10. Duplicate and Suspicious Claims
10.1 Submitting the same expense more than once is prohibited.
10.2 Possible duplicates must be reviewed before approval.
